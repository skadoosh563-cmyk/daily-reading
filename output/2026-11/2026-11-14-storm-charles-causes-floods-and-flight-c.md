Heavy rain and strong south-westerly winds arrived first in the Portuguese island of Madeira, before the system headed towards the mainland on 11 November. After causing widespread flooding in Spain and Portugal, the system moved on a north-easterly track through southern France and then towards northern Italy. The Italian weather service issued a red rainfall warning, its highest level, for the area extending from Tuscany to Venezia. Further heavy rain is expected to fall in Italy and the Balkans over the next few days, with the latest forecast models indicating an additional 100mm could accumulate widely, while totals close to 200mm are likely in more mountainous areas.

Successive rounds of heavy rain and strong winds have caused flooding, landslides and disruption across British Columbia, Canada through the last week or so.

Between Monday and Friday last week, severe rainfall and strong winds left more than 400,000 customers without power in British Columbia. These unsettled conditions continued into the weekend, with further heavy rain leaving 135,000 customers without power, alongside downed trees, flooded roads and landslides. Strong winds were also recorded on Sunday, with a gust of 72.1mph (116 km/h) on Herbert Island and a gust of 60mph (96 km/h) at Victoria international airport.

Heavy rain warnings were then issued on Wednesday for British Columbia’s Vancouver Island and southern coastal areas as multiple stations recorded rainfall totals in excess of 100mm. - 结构：同位语 + 现在分词短语作后置定语
- 讲解：“its highest level”是同位语，补充说明a red rainfall warning的级别；逗号隔开使信息更清晰。“extending from Tuscany to Venezia”是现在分词短语作后置定语，修饰the area，相当于which extends...，使句子简洁流畅。
