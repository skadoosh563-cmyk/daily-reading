## 二、原文

OpenAI won’t face criminal penalties over the Medicare hack but the prime minister has flagged changes to Australia’s criminal law to crack down on future breaches by artificial intelligence companies.

Anthony Albanese on Tuesday released the recommendations of a “rapid review” into the June breach but not the findings or the report itself.

A key recommendation is for the attorney general’s department, the Australian federal police and home affairs to consider reforming the law to introduce “penalties for egregious regulatory non-compliance” and a “failure to prevent” offence which would criminalise developing an AI model that engaged in harmful conduct while failing “to take reasonable steps to prevent that harm”.

The review recommended the federal government “consider mandatory, time-bound AI safety reporting, including but not limited to misaligned behaviour or agentic cyber incidents”.

The home affairs minister, Tony Burke, told reporters “we need to make sure that our laws and our systems keep pace with the technology and also make sure that we’re able to use the technology to find vulnerabilities before others do”.

“The mandatory reporting, when artificial intelligence goes rogue, gives us a chance to be able to make sure that when this happens, the reporting occurs and occurs immediately,” Burke said in Canberra alongside the PM.

On the “concept of looking at how we can update our criminal law”, Burke said: “It should never be the case that there’s an argument that someone didn’t know the computer was loaded.”

“We need to make sure that the criminal law is up to date for how this new technology is able to act.”

Albanese on Tuesday characterised OpenAI’s breach of Services Australia as “minor” in terms of the implications for personal privacy.

“The clear indication is that no information was got that involves people’s personal details,” the prime minister told reporters in Canberra.

“All of that was confirmed essentially … but that doesn’t mean that it wasn’t a serious issue and that in other areas there might be more serious implications.”

The review made 18 recommendations. Albanese said “we accept all of the recommendations and we’ve commenced work to implement them”.

Albanese did not directly answer a question about whether the OpenAI incident in June would have breached the proposed new laws if they were in place.

AI researcher Toby Walsh said the people in charge of these companies needed to be criminally liable to ensure they did the right thing.

“We’ve tried fining them, and we’re not the only ones. These companies are incredibly wealthy, and it’s almost impossible to fine them enough,” he said.

The AI agent developed by OpenAI accessed Services Australia data and three other systems in June.

The company didn’t notify the Australian government until mid-September despite becoming aware of the incident itself in August.

Guardian Australia revealed last month OpenAI used AI to help write the email to notify Australian authorities.

Albanese said the government would set up a new AI safety regulator.

Labor would consider six key obligations on AI companies, including:

- maintaining security controls across all stages of development and deployment;

- identify and manage serious risks associated with their models;

- provide mandatory notification of incidents to the Australian government rapidly;

- allow independent evaluation of their models;

- maintain and publish safety and security frameworks to ensure good governance, risk management, and transparency;

- and respond to updated standards and rules as technologies change.

- 结构：介词短语作表语 + 不定式复合结构 + 定语从句嵌套
- 讲解：主句是“A key recommendation is for... to consider...”，用“for + 名词 + to do”结构作表语，说明建议的内容。后面“which would criminalise...”是定语从句修饰“offence”，其中又嵌套了“that engaged in...”定语从句修饰“AI model”，以及“while failing...”作伴随状语。这种多层嵌套在政策类外刊中很常见，阅读时先抓主干“recommendation is for sb. to consider doing”，再逐层看修饰成分。

**2）“It should never be the case that there’s an argument that someone didn’t know the computer was loaded.”**

- 结构：形式主语 + 同位语从句 + 虚拟语气
- 讲解：“It should never be the case that...”用形式主语it，真正的主语是that从句，表示“绝不应该出现……的情况”。其中“that there’s an argument that...”又是同位语从句，解释argument的内容。“should never be”带有强烈的规范语气，相当于“绝不允许”。这种句式在表达法律或道德立场时很有力，比直接说“Someone should never argue...”更正式。

**3）“We’ve tried fining them, and we’re not the only ones. These companies are incredibly wealthy, and it’s almost impossible to fine them enough,” he said.**

- 结构：现在完成时 + 并列句 + 形式主语
- 讲解：“We’ve tried fining them”用现在完成时表示“已经尝试过罚款这种方式”，暗示效果不佳。“we’re not the only ones”是省略表达，完整为“we’re not the only ones who have tried fining them”，避免重复。后一句“it’s almost impossible to fine them enough”用形式主语it，真正主语是不定式，强调“罚款罚到足够”这件事几乎不可能。口语中这种省略和形式主语很常见，值得模仿。
