## 二、原文

Anthropic set the share price for its debut on the US stock market at $83 on Wednesday, positioning the artificial intelligence company for one of the biggest initial public offerings in history. The firm is slated to begin trading on Thursday under the ticker symbol ANT, with CEO Dario Amodei and other co-founders ringing the bell to open the Nasdaq exchange.

The pricing is above Anthropic’s earlier estimates. Last week, the San Francisco-based company, founded in 2021, set its preliminary share price at $70 to $78. With all shares counted, those prices put Anthropic’s initial public offering (IPO) from $1.26tn to $1.41tn. Elon Musk’s SpaceX debuted on Wall Street in June with the biggest market offering ever at $1.77tn. With Anthropic setting its share price at $83, it could now surpass SpaceX.

Amodei is planning to sell about $260m worth of shares with the IPO, as are his six fellow co-founders. At the top of the price range, the co-founders’ stakes will be worth more than $18bn each.

When Anthropic filed for its IPO last month, the company detailed rapidly rising revenue in its prospectus: $41m in 2023, $386m in 2024, $4.585bn in 2025 (an increase of more than 1,000%), then $16.286bn in the first six months of 2026 alone. The company disclosed that, as of the end of June, it had raised $141.1bn.

Despite the increasing revenue, Anthropic disclosed a net loss of $80bn for the first half of 2026.

Anthropic’s main expenditure listed in its investor prospectus is computing power from datacenters. The company has compute purchase commitments and lease agreements of more than $70bn over the next 12 months and more than $450bn by 2030, with a further $450bn due by 2045.

Anthropic is the first company at the forefront of the generative AI boom to go public, and its performance is likely to affect investors’ enthusiasm. Its competitor OpenAI was slated to hit Wall Street later this year, but CEO Sam Altman has said that the company opted to delay its IPO until it can overcome safety concerns.

Anthropic’s IPO comes during a volatile time for AI and the stock market. Last month, the industry saw a broader slowdown, with share prices dropping at several companies amid growing concern about the technology. Then, last week, Nvidia, which designs the chips AI companies use to power their chatbots, was racked with a scandal that led to CEO Jensen Huang resigning after reports of multiple extramarital affairs.

## 五、长难句拆解

**1）The firm is slated to begin trading on Thursday under the ticker symbol ANT, with CEO Dario Amodei and other co-founders ringing the bell to open the Nasdaq exchange.**

- 结构：with 复合结构（with + 名词 + 现在分词）作伴随状语
- 讲解：“with + 宾语 + 分词”是外刊极常见的收尾方式：它把两个独立的主谓信息压缩进一个从句，避免再起一个句子。这里 CEO 和创始人们是“主动”敲钟，所以用现在分词 ringing；如果是被动关系就用过去分词（第二段的 With all shares counted 正是如此）。翻译时通常处理成并列小句：“……，CEO 将敲响开市钟”。

**2）Amodei is planning to sell about $260m worth of shares with the IPO, as are his six fellow co-founders.**

- 结构：as 引导的倒装（as + 助动词 + 主语），表示“某某也是如此”
- 讲解：肯定句后用 as is/are/does + 主语，表示前面说的情况同样适用于后者；否定则用 neither/nor。这里必须倒装，因为 as 在句首作“也同样”讲，不倒装就变成“正如他的六位联合创始人（是……）”的定语从句，意思走偏。写作中想避免重复整个谓语时，这是最经济的办法。

**3）Then, last week, Nvidia, which designs the chips AI companies use to power their chatbots, was racked with a scandal that led to CEO Jensen Huang resigning after reports of multiple extramarital affairs.**

- 结构：非限制性定语从句 + 省略关系词的定语从句 + lead to sb doing 的嵌套
- 讲解：主干只有 Nvidia ... was racked with a scandal（英伟达陷入丑闻）。which 从句插在主语后补充说明英伟达是做什么的；其中 the chips AI companies use 是省略了 that/which 的定语从句（先行词 chips 在从句里作 use 的宾语，所以可以省略关系词）；后面的 that led to ... 修饰 scandal，lead to 后接动名词表示“导致某事发生”。读长句时先划掉两个逗号之间的插入成分，主干就出来了——这是应对财经类长句最实用的方法。

## 六、理解题

**1.** Which of the following best summarises the article? (A) Anthropic has become the most valuable AI company in the world. (B) Anthropic has priced its IPO above expectations, and the article sets out its finances and the market conditions surrounding the listing. (C) The article argues that OpenAI should have gone public before Anthropic. (D) The article explains why Nvidia is losing ground to Anthropic.

> 参考答案：B。文章主线是“定价 83 美元、高于预估区间、规模可能超过 SpaceX”，随后用招股书数字（营收、净亏损、算力支出）和行业环境（AI 板块放缓、英伟达丑闻）补充背景。A 不对：文中没有说它已是市值最高的 AI 公司，只说它的 IPO 规模可能超过 SpaceX。C 是文章提到的对比信息，但不是主旨。D 属过度推断，英伟达的丑闻与 Anthropic 之间没有竞争关系。

**2.** What can be inferred from the financial figures in paragraphs 4–6 about Anthropic's business?

> 参考答案：可以推断出三点：第一，它增长极快，2026 年上半年营收（162.86 亿美元）就已经远超 2025 年全年（45.85 亿美元）。第二，它烧钱速度更快——上半年净亏损 800 亿美元，远高于同期营收，说明成本（主要是数据中心算力）远超收入，规模扩张尚未带来盈利。第三，它的支出压力是长期的：未来 12 个月算力采购与租赁承诺超过 700 亿美元，到 2030 年超过 4500 亿美元，2045 年前还要再付 4500 亿美元，意味着未来很长一段时间都要靠融资和上市输血。

**3.** What does the final paragraph suggest about the timing of Anthropic's IPO?

> 参考答案：作者的倾向是“时机并不理想、风险偏大”，但语气克制、不直接评论。依据是：作者用 volatile time 概括当下环境；用 a broader slowdown、share prices dropping、growing concern 说明 AI 板块整体在回调；又举英伟达 CEO 因丑闻辞职的极端例子，进一步渲染行业情绪不稳。把这些放在全文结尾，等于给前面“估值可能超过 SpaceX”的乐观叙述加了一个对冲：作为首家上市的生成式 AI 前沿公司，它的股价表现会被放在这种疑虑情绪中检验。
