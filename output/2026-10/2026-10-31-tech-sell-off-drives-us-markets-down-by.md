## 二、原文

A sell-off of tech stocks that began earlier this month and gained steam with disappointing earnings from leading tech companies drove US markets down by hundreds of billions of dollars Friday. In total, the decline has knocked nearly $1tn in value off the tech-heavy Nasdaq exchange in one week and erased about $2tn in a month.

The S&P 500, the index of the 500 most valuable companies in the US, fell by more than 1% Friday, losing hundreds of billions of dollars in market value. It was on track to erase its gain for October, even though it set its all-time high during the same month. The weakness for technology stocks dragged the Nasdaq composite down even further, more than 3%, also erasing hundreds of billions in value. The Dow Jones Industrial Average fell less.

More than $2tn in market value is set to be wiped from the Nasdaq 100 this month, as big tech earnings intensified investor scrutiny of whether the billions being poured into artificial intelligence can generate returns quickly enough.

The wipeout would amount to slightly more than SpaceX’s entire market value. The index has fallen more than 5.8% so far in October as the AI trade ran into turbulence, with hyperscalers Meta Platforms, Alphabet and Amazon raising spending forecasts further as they race to expand AI infrastructure.

Intel tumbled nearly 30% and is flirting with its worst day since 1974, even though the chipmaker reported a stronger profit for the latest quarter than analysts expected. It gave a forecast for profit in the final three months of this year that fell far short of analysts’ expectations.

Amazon fell 1.8% after reporting a weaker profit for the summer than analysts expected. The company also disclosed that it had spent more this year than anticipated.

A day earlier, Alphabet’s stock dropped more than 12%, wiping $500bn from its market capitalization, to its worst day on record after telling investors that it burned through more cash during the summer than the spring because of similar, mammoth investments in AI.

Worries are rising about whether all those hundreds of billions of dollars will produce enough profit and productivity to make AI investment worth it. At the same time, stocks of computer chip companies and other winners from the frenzy to build AI datacenters have come under pressure on concerns that high interest rates may slow the further build out of data centers.

A drop of 3.1% for Nvidia, previously worth about $5tn, whose chips have made it the poster child of the AI frenzy, was the heaviest force pulling the S&P 500 downward.

Such losses overshadowed an otherwise mixed day for the rest of Wall Street, with the S&P 500 roughly evenly split between gainers and losers.

Expectations are high broadly for US companies’ ability to make bigger profits, even though people around the world are feeling discouraged about high inflation and high fuel prices. Such expectations are what drove the S&P 500 to its record earlier this month.

**1）A sell-off of tech stocks that began earlier this month and gained steam with disappointing earnings from leading tech companies drove US markets down by hundreds of billions of dollars Friday.**

- 讲解：主干是 A sell-off drove US markets down。that began...and gained steam... 是修饰 sell-off 的定语从句，因为主语后面跟了很长的修饰成分，真正谓语 drove 出现得较晚，阅读时要抓住主干。这种写法让一句话承载了时间、原因等多层信息，是新闻英语的典型压缩手法。

**2）More than $2tn in market value is set to be wiped from the Nasdaq 100 this month, as big tech earnings intensified investor scrutiny of whether the billions being poured into artificial intelligence can generate returns quickly enough.**

- 结构：被动语态 + as 原因状语从句 + whether 宾语从句 + 现在分词作后置定语
- 讲解：主句用被动 is set to be wiped，强调“市值将被蒸发”这一结果；as 引导原因状语，说明背景；of whether... 是 scrutiny 的同位语/介词宾语从句，解释审视的内容；being poured into... 是现在分词短语作后置定语修饰 the billions，表示“正在被投入的数十亿资金”。多层嵌套让句子信息密度很高，是财经报道分析因果时的常见句式。

**3）A drop of 3.1% for Nvidia, previously worth about $5tn, whose chips have made it the poster child of the AI frenzy, was the heaviest force pulling the S&P 500 downward.**

- 结构：同位语 + 非限制性定语从句 + 现在分词作后置定语
- 讲解：主语是 A drop of 3.1% for Nvidia，后面插入了 previously worth about $5tn（形容词短语作补充说明）和 whose chips...（非限制性定语从句），谓语 was 被推后。pulling the S&P 500 downward 是现在分词短语作后置定语修饰 force。这种写法在主语后插入大量背景信息，既补充了英伟达的市值和地位，又不打断主句节奏，阅读时要注意识别插入成分的边界。
